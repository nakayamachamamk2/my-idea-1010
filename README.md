## About

### Make hobby money more fun

Many people earn a little from their hobbies, but it is hard to see progress. Hobby Meter makes it feel like a game.

- For people who earn pocket money from hobbies
- Set one goal for the month
- See your progress at a glance
- Make your favorite things even more fun

### How it works

Set a monthly goal, add income when you earn it, and watch the round meter fill up.

- Set your goal in yen and edit it anytime
- Tap "Add income" and pick a type: handmade, photos, illustration or other
- Type an amount or tap quick buttons like +¥500
- The round meter shows your percent and how much is left

### Why it matters, what's next

Small wins keep you going. Fun rewards help you stay motivated all month.

- Confetti, badges and a chick that grows keep it fun
- Badges and a history list show your steady effort
- Works in English and Japanese
- Next: choose the look and feel, such as colors and mood
